# Athens

Athens is the capital of Greece and one of the world's oldest cities. In antiquity it was the preeminent city-state of ancient Greece, renowned as a centre of philosophy, democracy, and culture.

## Role in the Paradox

According to [Plutarch](../entities/plutarch.md), the Athenians preserved the ship of [Theseus](../entities/theseus.md) in Athens as a monument, replacing its planks over time as they decayed. This act of preservation is the original context for the [Ship of Theseus / Identity Paradox](../concepts/ship-of-theseus-identity-paradox.md).

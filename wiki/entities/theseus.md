# Theseus

Theseus is a figure from Greek mythology, most famous as the hero who slew the Minotaur. In the context of philosophy, his name is attached to one of the most enduring thought experiments about identity and change: the [Ship of Theseus / Identity Paradox](../concepts/ship-of-theseus-identity-paradox.md).

## Role in the Paradox

According to [Plutarch](../entities/plutarch.md), the Athenians preserved Theseus's ship in [Athens](../entities/athens.md) as a monument after his legendary voyage. As the wooden planks decayed over time, they were replaced one by one — setting the stage for the paradox that now bears his name.

# Derek Parfit

## Description

Derek Parfit (1942–2017) was a British moral philosopher and metaphysician based at All Souls College, Oxford. He is considered one of the most important analytic philosophers of the twentieth century. His landmark work *Reasons and Persons* (1984) transformed debates in personal identity, rationality, and ethics. On personal identity, Parfit argued that strict numerical identity — the notion that a person at one time is literally the same entity as a person at another time — matters less than psychological connectedness and continuity. His reductionist view holds that what matters for survival is the continuation of the mental pattern, not the persistence of some further fact about the self.

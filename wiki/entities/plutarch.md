# Plutarch

Plutarch was a Greek biographer, essayist, and Middle Platonist philosopher (c. 46–119 AD). He is best known for his *Parallel Lives*, a series of biographies pairing famous Greeks and Romans. In the context of the wiki, he is the primary ancient source for the Ship of Theseus thought experiment.

## Role in the Paradox

Plutarch described how the Athenians preserved the ship of [Theseus](../entities/theseus.md) in [Athens](../entities/athens.md), replacing decayed planks over time. This account gave philosophers the raw material for the [Ship of Theseus / Identity Paradox](../concepts/ship-of-theseus-identity-paradox.md).

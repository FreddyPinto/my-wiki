# John Locke

## Description

John Locke (1632–1704) was an English philosopher and physician, widely regarded as one of the most influential Enlightenment thinkers. He made foundational contributions to epistemology, political philosophy, and the philosophy of mind. In epistemology he is known for the theory that the mind begins as a blank slate (*tabula rasa*), shaped entirely by experience. In political philosophy, his *Two Treatises of Government* (1689) established the basis for liberal democracy and the social contract tradition. In the philosophy of personal identity, his *Essay Concerning Human Understanding* (1689) introduced the memory-based account of the self that became the starting point for all subsequent debate.

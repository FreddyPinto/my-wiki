# Thomas Hobbes

Thomas Hobbes (1588–1679) was an English philosopher, best known for his political treatise *Leviathan* (1651). He is considered one of the founders of modern political philosophy and social contract theory.

## Role in the Paradox

Hobbes added a crucial second stage to the [Ship of Theseus / Identity Paradox](../concepts/ship-of-theseus-identity-paradox.md): he posed that if someone collected all the discarded original planks and reassembled them into a second ship, both vessels would have a claim to being the "real" Ship of [Theseus](../entities/theseus.md). This twist — sometimes called the Hobbesian Twist — sharpens the paradox by introducing two competing objects rather than one gradually changing ship.
